Why WasteWise

Different from Every Existing Approach

WasteWise is not simply a forecasting tool, a waste tracker or a surplus-resale platform. It focuses on the production decision itself — before any food is prepared.

Traditional Forecasting

"What are we likely to sell?"

Estimates future demand using historical sales data. Tells operators what customers may buy — but does not convert this into a production quantity.

e.g. Tenzo — restaurant analytics and forecasting.

Waste Measurement

"What did we waste?"

Tracks and analyses food waste after it occurs. Helps businesses understand their waste patterns retrospectively but does not address the production decision itself.

e.g. Winnow / Leanpath — primarily measure waste after it occurs.

Surplus Resale

"How can we sell what is left?"

Helps businesses recover value from surplus food that was already over-produced. Does not address the production decision itself.

e.g. Too Good To Go — helps recover value from surplus food.

WasteWise Approach

WasteWise

"What should we actually produce before waste occurs?"

Converts demand forecasts into waste-aware production quantities — before food is prepared. Considers waste risk, shelf life, batch size and product economics to generate an actionable recommendation.
